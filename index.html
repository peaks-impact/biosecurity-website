Our mission

We want more people in Switzerland working on the biological risks that could cause harm at a global scale.

Biotechnology has become cheaper and more capable over the past two decades, and AI systems are now able to assist with parts of it. That changes the risk picture: a pathogen causing a pandemic no longer has to come from nature. It could come from a laboratory accident, or from someone who intended it.

Work on this problem is not short of ideas. Sequencing-based detection, screening of synthesis orders, better protective equipment and clearer norms for publication are all being developed, mostly by small groups. What limits progress is how few people work on them. Switzerland has the relevant biology and engineering departments, a large life sciences industry, and in Geneva the organisations that negotiate biological weapons policy. We want to bring more people into this field.

Forecasters' and domain experts' estimate that an engineered pathogen kills more than 1% of humanity by 2100.
