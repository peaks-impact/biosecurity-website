Who we are

The Zurich Biosecurity Hub is a small group that meets in Zurich. We read papers, we bring in speakers, and we help people who want to work on biological risk work out where to start.

There is no membership and nothing to sign. Come to a talk and see whether it is for you!

Fellowship

A structured course on globally catastrophic biological risks for a small group. It works through the field in sequence: how a pandemic could be engineered rather than emerge, what AI changes in the laboratory, how outbreaks are detected, how synthesis orders are screened, which countermeasures work whatever the pathogen, and how all of this is governed.

Each unit pairs reading you do on your own with a facilitated discussion in the group, so everyone arrives with the same material and the time together goes into questions rather than summaries. The curriculum is adapted from the BlueDot Impact biosecurity course. No background in biology is needed; the reading is chosen to work for people from engineering, medicine, computer science and policy.

Applications are open. Dates, format and how to apply are on our Luma calendar, where the fellowship is listed with the other events.

Paper reading group

The reading group has been meeting for about a year and is the oldest part of the Hub. We meet roughly once a month at the University of Zurich Irchel, and you can join online if you cannot be there.

Each session takes one paper or report, circulated in advance. One person introduces it briefly, then the rest of the time is discussion: what the authors claim, whether the evidence holds up, and what it would mean for policy or for work in a lab. Sessions this autumn cover barriers to bioweapons development and the Biological Weapons Convention.

Newcomers are welcome at any session, and reading the paper is the only preparation. Next meetings are on the calendar.

Career advising

One-to-one conversations about working on these problems: what to read, which programmes to apply for, who to talk to. To get started, introduce yourself in a short form so we know what you are working on, then write to info@zurichbiosecurity.ch and we will set up a conversation.

Coworking space

The Hub is based at PEAKS, a shared office at Zeughausstrasse 31 in Zurich, a short walk from the main station and close to ETH and the University of Zurich. It is a coworking space for people working on the largest risks: AI safety researchers, independent researchers and visitors passing through, and now the Zurich Biosecurity Hub.

Sitting among people who work on adjacent problems is part of the point. If you work on biosecurity and want a desk, or want to see the place first, PEAKS runs public coworking days on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Details and sign-up are on the PEAKS website, or write to us and come along with someone from the Hub.
